Process

How a mandate becomes an introduction

Five steps. Nobody meets anybody until step four, and step four only opens when the file is complete on both sides.

01

Express interest

Name the mandate, your role and your capacity. One short form, no fee.

02

NDA & KYC

A mutual non-disclosure agreement and identity checks on the principal, not the intermediary.

03

Documentation

Seller: title, mandate letter, approvals. Buyer: proof of funds or term sheet, letter of intent.

Gate · both files complete
04

Introduction

Highline introduces the principals in writing and attends the first meeting.

05

Diligence & close

The parties negotiate directly, with their own advisers. Highline stays on the file until signing.

Standards

What we check, and what we promise

From sellers

  • Clear title or a written chain of mandate to the owner
  • Encumbrance, zoning and approval status, disclosed upfront
  • A signed mandate letter naming Highline Broadway Capital

From buyers & funders

  • KYC on the principal and the investing entity
  • Proof of funds, a bank comfort letter or a term sheet
  • A letter of intent on the specific mandate

From Highline

  • Your identity is never shared without your written consent
  • No circulation of documents beyond the parties who signed
  • One point of contact from first enquiry to close
